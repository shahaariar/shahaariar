## About me

I am an **M.S. student in Engineering Technology** and a **Graduate Teaching Assistant at Middle Tennessee State University (MTSU)**. I am a graduate student member of the **[Autonomous Systems and Quantum Intelligence (ASQI) Laboratory](https://ma-hossain.github.io/lab)**, directed by **[Dr. Mohammad Arif Hossain](https://ma-hossain.github.io/)**. My research centers on **antenna design, RF power harvesting, wireless power transmission, and rectenna systems**.

## Research foundation

My undergraduate thesis, **“Design and Analysis of Rectennas for RF Energy Harvesting,”** included:

- A dual-band antenna for RF power harvesting at **5.41 GHz and 7.36 GHz**
- Half-wave and three-stage Dickson rectifier development
- Rectifier-topology evaluation using **CST Studio Suite** and **Keysight ADS**
- Antenna equivalent-circuit design for rectenna integration
