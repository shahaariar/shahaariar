01 / About

Building efficient RF systems for a more connected world.

I am an M.S. student in Engineering Technology and a Graduate Teaching Assistant at Middle Tennessee State University. I am a graduate student member of the Autonomous Systems and Quantum Intelligence (ASQI) Laboratory, directed by Dr. Mohammad Arif Hossain.

My work brings together electromagnetic design, RF energy harvesting, and intelligent systems for next-generation wireless applications.
